Invisalign®: The Clear Alternative to Braces

Straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably with custom-made, removable clear aligners.

What is Invisalign®?

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth without the use of traditional metal braces. Instead, it utilizes a series of custom-made, virtually invisible, and removable aligners made of a smooth, comfortable plastic. Each aligner in the series is precisely calibrated to make slight adjustments to the position of your teeth. By switching to a new set of aligners every 1-2 weeks as prescribed by your orthodontist, your teeth gradually and gently move towards their projected final position, resulting in a beautiful, straight smile.

What Orthodontic Issues Can Invisalign Correct?

The Invisalign system is highly effective at treating a wide range of mild to moderate dental alignment issues, including:

  • Crowded Teeth: When there isn't enough room in your jaw for your teeth to fit normally.
  • Gapped Teeth: To close unwanted spaces and gaps between teeth.
  • Overbite: When the upper front teeth bite too far over the lower teeth.
  • Underbite: When the lower teeth protrude past the upper front teeth.
  • Crossbite: When some of your upper teeth bite on the inside of your lower teeth.
  • For very complex bite issues, traditional braces may still be the recommended option.

Are You a Good Candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign is an excellent choice for many teens and adults. The ideal candidate is someone who:

  • Is looking for a discreet and aesthetically pleasing way to straighten their teeth.
  • Has mild to moderate orthodontic problems.
  • Is highly disciplined and committed to wearing the aligners for the prescribed 20-22 hours per day.
  • Has excellent oral hygiene habits.

The Invisalign Treatment Process: From Scan to Smile

The Invisalign journey is powered by advanced 3D imaging technology.

Step 1: Consultation and 3D Scan

Your orthodontist will evaluate your smile and, if you are a candidate, take a fast and precise 3D digital scan of your teeth using a scanner like the iTero® element. This avoids the need for messy traditional impressions.

Step 2: The Custom Treatment Plan (ClinCheck®)

Using the 3D scan, your orthodontist will map out a precise, step-by-step digital treatment plan. This technology allows you to see a virtual representation of how your teeth will move throughout the treatment and even preview your new smile.

Step 3: Wearing Your Aligners

You will receive your series of custom-made aligners. You'll wear each set for 20-22 hours a day, switching to a new set every 1-2 weeks, as directed. You'll have periodic check-ups with your orthodontist every 6-8 weeks to monitor your progress.

Step 4: Maintaining Your New Smile

After you've completed your last set of aligners, you will need to wear a retainer (such as a Vivera® retainer) to protect your investment and prevent your teeth from gradually shifting back to their initial positions.

Benefits and Considerations of Invisalign

Advantages (Pros)

  • Virtually Invisible: The clear aligners are very discreet, so most people won't even notice you're undergoing treatment.
  • Removable: You can take your aligners out to eat, drink, brush, and floss. This means no dietary restrictions and it's easy to maintain good oral hygiene.
  • Comfortable: The smooth plastic aligners have no sharp metal brackets or wires that can irritate your cheeks and gums.
  • Predictable Results: The digital planning process allows you to see your projected results before you even start.

Considerations (Cons)

  • Requires Discipline: Success is entirely dependent on you wearing the aligners for the recommended 20-22 hours a day.
  • Must Be Removed for Eating/Drinking: You must take them out for all meals and any drinks other than cool water.
  • Can Be Misplaced: Because they are removable, there is a risk of losing or misplacing your aligners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Invisalign treatment hurt?

You will likely feel pressure and mild discomfort for the first few days of wearing a new set of aligners. This is a normal and temporary sensation—it's a sign that the aligners are actively moving your teeth. The discomfort is usually much less than that associated with tightening traditional braces.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

The average treatment time for an adult is about 12 to 18 months, though it can be shorter or longer depending on the complexity of your case. Your orthodontist will provide you with an estimated treatment length based on your custom digital plan.

How do I clean my aligners?

You can clean your aligners by brushing them gently with a soft toothbrush and cool water. You can also use a specialized aligner cleaning solution or retainer cleaner. It's important to clean them regularly to keep them fresh and invisible.
