Thigh Lift: A Guide to Thighplasty Surgery

Achieve firmer, more youthful-looking thighs by surgically removing excess, sagging skin.

What is a Thigh Lift?

A thigh lift, known medically as a thighplasty, is a surgical body contouring procedure designed to reshape the thighs by removing excess, sagging skin and, in some cases, fat. The primary goal is to create smoother skin and a more proportionate, toned contour for the thighs and lower body. It is a powerful procedure for patients who are left with significant skin laxity after major weight loss or due to the natural effects of aging. It is important to understand that a thigh lift is a skin removal procedure, not a weight loss tool.

Why Consider a Thigh Lift?

Patients pursue a thighplasty to address a range of functional and aesthetic concerns.

  • You have significant loose, sagging, or "flabby" skin on your inner or outer thighs.
  • This is a common concern for patients after massive weight loss from bariatric surgery or diet and exercise.
  • Your skin has lost its elasticity due to the natural aging process.
  • The appearance of your thighs does not improve despite maintaining a stable, healthy weight.
  • The excess skin causes physical discomfort, such as chafing, rashes, or hygiene issues.
  • You feel self-conscious or uncomfortable wearing shorts, swimwear, or fitted pants.

Types of Thigh Lift Procedures

The type of thigh lift depends on the location and amount of excess skin to be removed.

Inner (Medial) Thigh Lift

This is the most common type of thighplasty, targeting the loose skin on the inner thighs. The incision is typically placed in the natural crease of the groin, and may extend downward and wrap around to the back of the thigh, depending on the amount of skin to be removed.

Outer (Lateral) Thigh Lift

This procedure addresses sagging on the front and outer aspect of the thighs. The incisions are generally more extensive, often starting in the groin and extending around the hip. An outer thigh lift is frequently combined with other procedures like a tummy tuck or a lower body lift for comprehensive contouring.

Mini Thigh Lift

This is a less invasive option for patients who have a minimal amount of sagging skin, confined only to the upper third of the inner thigh. It involves a shorter incision placed discreetly within the groin crease.

The Thighplasty Journey: Surgery to Recovery

The Surgical Procedure

A thigh lift is performed under general anesthesia. Your surgeon will make the planned incisions, lift the underlying supportive tissue, remove a predetermined amount of excess skin and fat, and then re-drape the skin smoothly over the new, tighter contour. Liposuction is often used in conjunction with the lift to further refine the shape. The incisions are then closed in layers with sutures.

Recovery and Healing Timeline

Recovery from a thigh lift is significant and requires patience and strict adherence to post-operative instructions.

Weeks 1-2
This is the most challenging phase of recovery. You will experience significant swelling, bruising, and discomfort. Surgical drains are often in place to remove excess fluid. A compression garment must be worn continuously, and your mobility will be very limited.
Weeks 2-4
You can often return to a desk job, but you will still be quite sore. Swelling and discomfort will gradually improve, but the compression garment is still mandatory. You must continue to avoid strenuous activity.
Weeks 6-8
Most surgeons will clear you to resume all normal activities, including most forms of exercise. Swelling will continue to improve for several more months.
Months 6-12
The final, refined results of your surgery will emerge as all residual swelling completely disappears and the scars begin to mature and fade.

Understanding the Scars and Risks

A thigh lift produces a permanent and significant scar. Patients must fully understand and accept this trade-off to achieve a tighter, smoother thigh contour. The length and visibility of the scar depend on the type of lift performed. Other potential risks include:

  • Asymmetry in shape or scarring.
  • Hematoma (a collection of blood) or seroma (a collection of fluid) under the skin.
  • Poor wound healing or incision separation, particularly in the delicate groin area.
  • Changes in skin sensation, such as numbness.
  • Major risks associated with any surgery, such as deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and infection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a thigh lift get rid of cellulite?

A thighplasty is not a treatment for cellulite. While the act of tightening the skin may incidentally improve the appearance of cellulite to some degree, the procedure is not designed to, and will not, eliminate it. Other specific cellulite treatments are required to address that issue.

Is a thigh lift a weight loss procedure?

No. A thigh lift is a body contouring procedure intended for people who are already at a stable and healthy weight but are left with excess, sagging skin. While the removed skin and fat do have weight, the procedure will not result in a significant change on the scale.

How long will my results last?

The results of a thigh lift are very long-lasting. The excess skin that is removed is gone forever. However, to maintain your new contour, it is crucial to maintain a stable weight through a healthy diet and exercise. The natural aging process will also continue, but your thighs will always look better than if you had not had the surgery.
