Carboxytherapy: A Guide to Carbon Dioxide Gas Treatment

Harness the power of CO2 to rejuvenate your skin, reduce cellulite, and fade stretch marks.

What is Carboxytherapy?

Carboxytherapy is a minimally invasive cosmetic treatment that involves the therapeutic use of medical-grade carbon dioxide (CO2) gas. During the procedure, a very small amount of CO2 is injected just beneath the skin's surface. The body interprets this as an oxygen deficit and responds by increasing blood flow and growth factors to the area. This surge in oxygen and nutrients stimulates circulation, encourages cell restoration, and increases the production of collagen, which can improve the skin's texture and appearance.

What Can Carboxytherapy Treat?

This versatile treatment can be used on the face and body to address a variety of concerns:

  • Cellulite Reduction: By improving circulation and damaging fat cells, carboxytherapy can help smooth the dimpled appearance of cellulite on the thighs and buttocks.
  • Stretch Marks (Striae): The new collagen formation stimulated by the treatment can help remodel and fade the appearance of both new and old stretch marks.
  • Dark Under-Eye Circles: For circles caused by poor circulation (vascular pooling), increasing oxygenated blood flow to the area can lighten the bluish tinge.
  • Skin Laxity & Rejuvenation: It can be used to stimulate collagen and tighten skin on the face, neck, arms, and abdomen.
  • Localized Fat Reduction: In some cases, high volumes of the gas can be used to break down fat cells in small, targeted areas like the jowls or under the chin.

Are You a Good Candidate for Carboxytherapy?

Carboxytherapy is suitable for most individuals seeking a non-surgical solution for common skin issues. A good candidate:

  • Is looking to improve the appearance of cellulite, stretch marks, or dark circles.
  • Desires subtle skin tightening without undergoing surgery.
  • Is in good general health.
  • Is not pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Has realistic expectations, understanding that multiple sessions are needed for optimal results.

The Carboxytherapy Treatment Process

The procedure is straightforward and performed in an office setting.

A tank containing medical-grade carbon dioxide is connected through tubing to a flow-regulating device. The gas is passed through a sterile filter and out through a very fine, tiny needle. Your provider will clean the treatment area and then perform a series of small injections to deliver the CO2 gas directly into the targeted tissue. Patients may feel a sensation of pressure or tingling as the gas enters the area.

The Carboxytherapy Journey: Sessions and Recovery

The Treatment Session

A single carboxytherapy session is quick, typically lasting only 15 to 30 minutes depending on the area being treated. It is a "lunchtime" procedure with no real downtime.

Recovery and Treatment Timeline

Recovery is minimal, allowing you to return to your daily activities immediately.

Immediately After Treatment
The treated area may feel warm, tingly, and slightly puffy. A faint crackling sensation under the skin is normal and disappears within minutes as the gas is absorbed.
First 24 Hours
Minor redness or bruising at the injection sites is possible but usually fades quickly. You can resume all normal activities. It is sometimes recommended to avoid bathing or swimming for a few hours post-treatment.
The Treatment Course
Carboxytherapy is not a one-time fix. A series of treatments is required for best results, typically scheduled once or twice a week for a total of 6-12 sessions.
Seeing Results
While some improvement in circulation may be felt quickly, visible results for collagen production and skin remodeling appear gradually over the course of the treatment series.

Understanding the Side Effects and Safety Profile

Carboxytherapy is considered a very safe procedure with a low risk of side effects, primarily because CO2 is a natural gas that is constantly produced and processed by the body. The risks are minimal and temporary:

  • The most common side effect is minor bruising at an injection site.
  • Slight swelling or redness that typically subsides within an hour or two.
  • A temporary crackling sensation under the skin as the gas absorbs.
  • A mild stinging or pressure-like discomfort during the injection itself.
  • There is no risk of an allergic reaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is carboxytherapy painful?

Most patients describe the sensation as a bit strange but not painful. You may feel pressure, warmth, or a mild stinging/tingling as the gas is injected. Any discomfort is very brief and stops as soon as the needle is removed.

How many sessions will I need?

The exact number depends on the condition and area being treated. For cellulite or significant stretch marks, a course of 6 to 12 sessions is often recommended. For dark under-eye circles or minor skin rejuvenation, 4 to 6 sessions may be sufficient.

How long do the results of carboxytherapy last?

The longevity of the results varies. For permanent conditions like scars and stretch marks, the improvement can be very long-lasting. For conditions influenced by aging and lifestyle, like cellulite and skin laxity, periodic maintenance sessions (e.g., every 6-12 months) are often recommended to preserve the benefits.
