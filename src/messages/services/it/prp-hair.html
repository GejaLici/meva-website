PRP for Hair Loss: A Natural Solution for Hair Regrowth

Stimulate your hair follicles and combat thinning with the regenerative power of Platelet-Rich Plasma.

What is PRP Therapy for Hair Loss?

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy for hair loss is a non-surgical medical treatment that utilizes the healing and regenerative properties of your own blood to stimulate hair follicles. The procedure involves drawing a small amount of your blood, processing it in a centrifuge to isolate and concentrate the platelets, and then injecting this PRP into the areas of thinning hair on your scalp. Platelets are rich in powerful growth factors. When injected into the scalp, these growth factors are believed to increase blood supply to the follicles, stimulate dormant or weakened follicles to enter an active growth phase, and prolong the growing phase of the hair cycle.

What Are the Benefits of PRP Hair Treatment?

PRP therapy is a popular choice for those seeking a natural approach to hair restoration:

  • Can effectively slow down the rate of hair thinning and reduce hair shedding.
  • Stimulates the regrowth of hair for increased density and coverage.
  • Increases the thickness and health of existing hair shafts, leading to fuller-looking hair.
  • A completely natural and safe procedure, as it uses your own biological material, eliminating the risk of allergic reactions.
  • Can be used as a standalone treatment or to enhance the results of other therapies, such as medications (Minoxidil, Finasteride) or a hair transplant surgery.

Are You a Good Candidate for PRP Hair Therapy?

PRP therapy is most effective for individuals with certain types of hair loss. You may be a good candidate if:

  • You are in the early to moderate stages of hair thinning or loss. It is most effective on areas with existing, but weakened, hair follicles.
  • Your hair loss is diagnosed as androgenetic alopecia (common male or female pattern baldness).
  • You are in good general health and do not have any underlying blood disorders or active scalp infections.
  • You understand that results are gradual and that a series of treatments is required for the best outcome.

The PRP Hair Treatment Procedure: A Step-by-Step Process

A PRP session is a straightforward, in-office procedure.

Step 1: The Blood Draw

A small vial of blood is drawn from your arm, just like a standard blood test.

Step 2: Centrifugation

The vial of blood is placed into a centrifuge. This machine spins at a very high speed, which separates the blood into its components: red blood cells, plasma, and a concentrated layer of platelets (the PRP).

Step 3: Scalp Injections

The scalp is thoroughly cleansed. To ensure comfort, your provider may use a topical numbing cream, local anesthetic, or a chilling device. The extracted PRP is then drawn into a syringe and meticulously injected in a grid-like pattern across the areas of thinning hair on the scalp using a very fine needle.

The Treatment Journey: Sessions and Results Timeline

The Treatment Course and Downtime

PRP is not a one-time treatment. An initial protocol typically consists of 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. There is no significant downtime, and you can typically resume your normal activities immediately, although your scalp may be tender for a day.

The Results Timeline

Patience is key, as results appear gradually.

Months 1-3
The first sign of success is often a noticeable decrease in hair shedding. Visible new growth is not typically seen yet.
Months 3-6
This is when most patients begin to see the first signs of new hair growth. Existing hairs may also start to feel thicker and healthier.
Maintenance
To maintain the results and continue to combat the progression of hair loss, follow-up or maintenance treatments are typically recommended every 6 to 12 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PRP hair treatment painful?

The scalp can be a sensitive area. Most clinics use effective methods to ensure comfort, such as topical numbing creams, local anesthetic injections, or chilling devices applied to the skin. While you may feel some pressure or a brief pinching sensation during the injections, the procedure is generally well-tolerated.

How effective is PRP for hair loss?

Many clinical studies have shown PRP to be an effective treatment for increasing hair count, hair thickness, and prolonging the growth phase of the hair cycle, particularly for androgenetic alopecia. Results can vary between individuals, and it tends to be most effective for those in the earlier stages of hair thinning.

Will PRP work on a completely bald spot?

PRP works by stimulating existing hair follicles that are dormant, miniaturized, or weakened. It cannot generate new hair follicles where they no longer exist. Therefore, it is generally not effective for areas that are completely bald ("slick bald"), but it is an excellent treatment for improving density in areas of thinning hair.
